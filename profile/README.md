## Reflect together. Move forward together.

Great work starts with honest conversations. **letRetro** gives teams a shared space to reflect on what happened, discover what matters, and turn those insights into action.

Whether you're running a sprint retrospective, learning from an incident, or bringing a team together around a challenge, letRetro helps make every conversation count.

## What you can do with letRetro

- **Run collaborative retrospectives** with templates, live feedback, voting, and comments.
- **Find the signal** with AI-assisted summaries, themes, and action items.
- **Follow through** by assigning action items and connecting work to tools such as Jira and GitHub.
- **Think visually** with a shared canvas for brainstorming and diagramming.
- **Keep improving** by exploring trends across retrospectives.

## Built for teams that care about how they work

We believe better teams are built through reflection, openness, and small improvements made consistently. We're building letRetro to make that practice easier for engineering, product, and agile teams.
